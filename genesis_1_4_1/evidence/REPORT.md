## Interpretation

The bridge encodes generic perceptual properties and observed outcomes. It supplies neither a mapping of old objects to new objects nor the live answer. The similarity weights are engineered. The ledger is reconstructed from past recorded observations rather than recovered by native memory recall. This tests whether those outcomes help a supplied bridge, and leaves spontaneous discovery of a cross-world representation open. Old stock observations are predictive. The archive contains effort costs, while the new workshop applies the charge after a delay. The archive does not establish that Omega previously learned a delayed cost.

Books are reachable scene objects with finite, sometimes incomplete or incorrect statements. The chosen Read action uses the motor's existing reach primitive and incurs energy and time costs. An estimate of source reliability can update from directly checked claims during practice. Read is a supplied affordance. Independent selection of that affordance is observable. This is separate from discovering how writing works.

All task outcomes use the same body and local menu. A rule-based motor proposal is the reference and receives the same modeled neural debit. Old Earth coordinates and partner IDs never score new objects. Three short practice instances per family precede held-out visual variants. The test varies seeded scenes within one procedural settlement, with fixed low-level dynamics.
